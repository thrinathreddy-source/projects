Nobody's watching

It works with the signal off.

Speech recognition, translation and speech run on hardware you own — your phone, or a small hub that costs about as much as a phone charger. A field with no bars is not a broken feature.

Which also means nothing you say leaves your hands. Not because of a policy you have to trust. Because there's nowhere for it to go.

One lens, on purpose

The other lens is just a lens.

Two displays cost twice as much, weigh more, and put a faint rectangle in front of both of your eyes. One display does everything text needs to do — and leaves the other side completely, ordinarily clear.

Cheaper, lighter, and harder to notice. Constraint as a feature, not a compromise.
